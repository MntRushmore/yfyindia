Youth for Yoga

We're a youth-led nonprofit on a mission to bring yoga to students everywhere — starting with an unforgettable journey to Ferozabad, India in 2026. We believe young people have the power to create real change.

Youth · Nonprofit · India · Yoga · Movement

Youth for Yoga started as a small teen-led initiative to share yoga with kids and has grown into a global movement promoting focus, resilience, physical health, and community among young people.

In 2026, we traveled to Ferozabad, Uttar Pradesh — visiting 5 schools, meeting students in their homes, and teaching 500+ young people the power of breath, movement, and presence.

From the classroom floor to the Taj Mahal, from tearful goodbyes to unforgettable hugs — this trip proved that yoga is universal, and that youth can build bridges across any distance.

Meet the youth leaders

Who We Are

Rushil Chopra

Co-Founder

Rushil is a teen wellness advocate who believes yoga has the power to transform lives. After seeing firsthand how stress and self-doubt affect young people, he co-founded Youth for Yoga to bring accessible, meaningful practice to students who need it most. In 2026, he led the team to Ferozabad, India — teaching yoga to 500+ girls across 5 schools. His vision: a world where every young person has the tools to feel grounded, confident, and heard.

Kashni Chopra

Co-Founder

Kashni co-founded Youth for Yoga driven by a deep belief in the connection between movement and self-expression. She has seen how yoga creates space for young women to discover their strength — not just physically, but emotionally. On the India Journey 2026, she worked alongside students and families in Ferozabad, building bonds that went far beyond the mat. Her vision: to grow Youth for Yoga into a movement that reaches girls everywhere, starting from the inside out.

Our Vision

We didn't start Youth for Yoga just to teach yoga.

We started it because we saw how many young people are struggling silently — with stress, pressure, and lack of emotional support. Our vision is to build a youth-led global movement, where teens don't just learn wellness — they lead it.
