We're Youth for Yoga — a youth-led nonprofit sharing the practice of yoga with students across India and beyond.

Our Mission

in Action

↓

Teaching Yoga

We led daily yoga sessions at schools across Ferozabad — working with students of all ages to build strength, focus, and calm.

Postures · Breathing · Mindfulness

Student Home Visits

We visited students in their homes, meeting families and understanding the communities we serve — making real, lasting connections.

Community · Connection · Care

Hugs & Lots of Love

The goodbyes that made everything worth it — students, families, and our team sharing moments of genuine connection and warmth at journey's end.

Connection · Joy · Gratitude

Taj Mahal & Beyond

From the Taj Mahal to local markets in Ferozabad — we immersed ourselves in the culture, history, and spirit of India.

India · Culture · Growth

Why This Matters

Young women in India face enormous pressure — academic, social, and emotional. Yoga gives them something most wellness programs don't: a practice they own. Not a test, not a grade — just breath, movement, and the knowledge that they are enough.

Mental health, confidence, and self-expression aren't extras. They're the foundation everything else is built on.

Mental Health · Confidence · Self-Expression · Girls
