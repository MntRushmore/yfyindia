Dear Grant Review Committee & Yoga Without Borders Team,

I am honored to support Yoga Without Borders, in collaboration with Rainbow Kids Yoga, EduGirls, and Yoga Gives Back.

As a yoga educator, wellness coach, and youth empowerment advocate based in California, U.S., I had the deeply humbling opportunity to volunteer at Dau Dayal Girls School in Firozabad in March, 2026. In just a few weeks, I witnessed students transform from hesitant and unsure to confident, expressive, and joyful, through simple practices like breathwork, partner yoga, and group activities. Within a short time, I noticed something shifted. I witnessed students:

  • Smiling more freely
  • Participating with enthusiasm
  • Supporting one another with kindness
  • Expressing themselves with growing confidence

One powerful moment was seeing students learn to ask, “Is this okay?” during partner work — building respect, consent, boundaries, and self-awareness in a meaningful way.

Yoga Without Borders goes beyond physical postures. It delivers confidence, empowerment, emotional resilience, and assertiveness, helping students find their voice, leadership, and connection — the skills that are critically needed today, especially in underserved communities. I believe that expanding this initiative to more schools globally can create a ripple effect, empowering children not only with health and wellness tools but with confidence, voice, and emotional strength that can shape their futures.

Yoga Without Borders is low-cost, scalable, and deeply impactful. I strongly believe it has the potential to reach and transform many more communities worldwide. This work is meaningful. It is needed. And it deserves to grow. I wholeheartedly support this grant application and the expansion of this meaningful work.

With gratitude,
Ruchi Chopra
