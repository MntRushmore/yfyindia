04 / Our Story

Youth for Yoga started as a small teen-led initiative to share yoga and has grown into a global movement promoting focus, resilience, and community.

In 2026, we traveled to Ferozabad, Uttar Pradesh — visiting 5 schools and teaching 500+ young people the power of breath, movement, and presence.

From the classroom floor to the Taj Mahal — this trip proved that yoga is universal, and that youth can build bridges across any distance.

09 / Why This Matters

Why This
Matters

Young women in India face enormous pressure — academic, social, and emotional. Yoga gives them something most wellness programs don't: a practice they own. Not a test, not a grade — just breath, movement, and the knowledge that they are enough.

Mental health, confidence, and self-expression aren't extras. They're the foundation everything else is built on.

Mental Health · Confidence · Self-Expression · Girls

11 / Who We Are

Meet the youth leaders

Who We Are

Rushil Chopra

Co-Founder

Rushil is a teen wellness advocate who believes yoga has the power to transform lives. After seeing firsthand how stress and self-doubt affect young people, he co-founded Youth for Yoga to bring accessible, meaningful practice to students who need it most. In 2026, he led the team to Ferozabad, India — teaching yoga to 500+ girls across 5 schools. His vision: a world where every young person has the tools to feel grounded, confident, and heard.

Kashni Chopra

Co-Founder

Kashni co-founded Youth for Yoga driven by a deep belief in the connection between movement and self-expression. She has seen how yoga creates space for young women to discover their strength — not just physically, but emotionally. On the India Journey 2026, she worked alongside students and families in Ferozabad, building bonds that went far beyond the mat. Her vision: to grow Youth for Yoga into a movement that reaches girls everywhere, starting from the inside out.

12 / Our Vision

Our Vision

We didn't start Youth for Yoga just to teach yoga.

We started it because we saw how many young people are struggling silently — with stress, pressure, and lack of emotional support. Our vision: a youth-led global movement where teens don't just learn wellness — they lead it.
