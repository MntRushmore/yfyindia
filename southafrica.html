What is Youth for Yoga?

A youth-led movement teaching wellness, by students, for students.

Youth for Yoga is a nonprofit started by two high school students in California. We bring yoga, breathwork, and mindfulness into schools that need it most, and train other students to lead the sessions themselves.

It's not about being flexible or knowing the names of poses. It's about having tools to handle stress. A way to find your voice. A community that grows stronger because you're in it.

In 2026, we travelled to India and taught over 500 students across three schools in Firozabad. Now we're bringing the movement to South Africa, starting with Parklands College.

Why It Matters

It's not about the poses.

Young people everywhere deal with stress, pressure, and self-doubt. We are rarely taught how to manage it. Even though we come from different countries, these challenges are the same.

01 — Confidence

Stay calm. Stay strong.

Breathing and mindfulness tools you carry with you for life. Manage stress before exams, before presentations, before anything.

02 — Voice

Find your voice.

Lead sessions for the students around you. Discover that you have something powerful to offer your community.

03 — Community

Build it together.

Real leadership. Real impact. Connect with student leaders across the world, all building the same thing.
